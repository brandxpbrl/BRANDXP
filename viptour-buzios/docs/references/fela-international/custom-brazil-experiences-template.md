# FELA TOURS INTERNATIONAL — Custom Brazil Experiences

Reusable proposal blueprint derived from the supplied family proposal. This is a blank structure, not a client proposal.

## 1. Cover

- FELA TOURS INTERNATIONAL — Custom Brazil Experiences
- Short positioning line
- Guest name / party label
- Travel window
- Destinations
- Proposal date and validity

## 2. Welcome and trip concept

- One concise paragraph describing the journey
- Travel style: private/shared, relaxed/balanced/full experience
- Occasion or personal context, when provided

## 3. Arrival and departure logistics

- Arrival airport, flight, date, and time
- Airport → accommodation transfer
- Departure accommodation → airport transfer
- Vehicle and luggage fit
- Driver/guide language
- Flight monitoring, waiting policy, and meeting instructions

## 4. Curated experiences

For each experience:

- Sequence number and title
- Destination / date placeholder
- Short editorial description
- What is included: transport, guide, admissions, equipment, meals, or concierge
- Duration / meeting point placeholder
- Operational notes and weather dependency
- Optional badge when it is not part of the core package

## 5. Optional experiences

Present optional items separately from the core itinerary. State clearly whether each item is subject to availability, weather, match schedule, or individual confirmation.

## 6. Concierge and personalization

- Restaurant reservations
- Birthday / anniversary coordination
- Special requests
- Accessibility, dietary, or mobility notes
- WhatsApp assistance and local coordination

## 7. Package summary

- Included services grouped in one scannable block
- Not included / assumptions
- Core package name
- Optional additions listed separately

## 8. Pricing presentation

Use confirmed values only:

- Price per person
- Number of travelers
- Family / group total
- Optional experience price per person or total
- Currency and payment terms
- What is included in the quoted amount
- Validity and availability disclaimer

Never carry prices, dates, phone numbers, or customer details from a previous proposal into a new one.

## 9. Confirmation and next step

- What the guest should confirm
- Availability / reservation conditions
- Payment instructions supplied at confirmation
- Contact and response channel

## Public implementation mapping

The public landing page expresses this structure through: arrival support, curated experiences, service levels, process, and a custom request form. A future proposal generator can map the sections above to a CMS or internal document workflow without publishing private proposal assets.
